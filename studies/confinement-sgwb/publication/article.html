How much did the measurements change the odds?

We evaluated two published-code grids of hidden confining sectors with three fermion flavors. Of 2,592 microscopic parameter combinations, 90 had positive thermal and wall solutions passing our perturbativity cut. The spread in log likelihood across either retained grid was at most 1.46 × 10−13. LVK thus assigned essentially the same likelihood to every tested spectrum.

For SU(4), a prior uniform in three squared masses and the decay constant puts the 95th percentile of the peak energy density at 1.76 × 10−18. Restricting the eta-prime squared mass to the lower three grid nodes raises that percentile to 3.27 × 10−18. Adding the observations preserves both values.

Why compare priors?

A November 2026 study found large prior dependence for phase-transition parameters, including an Abelian Higgs model. We test two finite grids with non-Abelian gauge groups and retain the normalization predicted by each model.

From detector limits to detector strain

Combining six public LVK cross spectra gives a flat-background 95% upper limit of 8.44 × 10−9 for a uniform amplitude prior. The collaboration reports 8.6 × 10−9. The corresponding log-uniform results are 2.78 and 2.8 × 10−9. The calculation fixes calibration and marginalizes a compact-binary foreground when testing confinement spectra.

Separately, twelve seconds of paired Hanford and Livingston strain before GW150914 exercise pygwb from calibrated time series through a geometry-corrected cross spectrum. A second Welch implementation agrees in relative L² norm to 0.043%. This short interval validates the processing path. The observing-run inference uses the collaboration’s released spectra.

How accurately do we know a model point?

A separate six-flavor spectrum agrees with a digitized journal curve to within 6.7% across 62 vertices. Re-solving one of the three-flavor grid points shifted its predicted peak from 5.88 to 6.78 × 10−21, or 15%. That numerical discrepancy matters for assigning precise amplitudes even though both values are far below detector reach.

Scope of the calculation

The six-flavor curve-to-row assignment is inferred. The three-flavor tables use archived transition solutions and the finite sound-wave lifetime convention. Elastic unitarity, a continuous parameter scan and a full observing-run strain reanalysis remain separate questions.
