# Prior art and scope

**Question, 22 November 2026.** For fixed non-Abelian confinement spectra, how much do LVK data change odds between microscopic grid points, and how much does changing the prior alter predicted peak amplitudes? This is a conditional replication and extension. Existing work already connects confining theories to gravitational waves and tests prior dependence in phase-transition inference.

| Closest work | What it establishes and how it relates |
|---|---|
| AbdusSalam, Kersten, Ojaghi, Velasco-Sevilla & Zafari, November 2026 | Uses O1–O4a SGWB products. Varies priors on spectral geometry, thermodynamics and Abelian Higgs parameters. Most parameters depend substantially on the prior. Here the signal amplitude follows from microscopic confinement coordinates and only the compact-binary foreground amplitude floats. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24625) |
| Houtz, Ulloa & West, JHEP 08 (2026) 059 | Computes finite-temperature effective potentials, wall velocities and spectra in non-Abelian dark sectors with fermions. Their LTE curves and publicly archived grid solutions supply the forward model. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08%282026%29059) · [Code](https://github.com/miarobin/Eta-Prime-GWs) |
| Huber, Phipps & Reichert, JHEP 07 (2026) 058 | Predicts generally weak pure SU(N) confinement signals using a lattice-informed Polyakov-loop model, surface tension and wall estimates. Pure Yang–Mills and the fermionic Houtz models have different dynamics. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14530) |
| Huang, Reichert, Sannino & Wang, Phys. Rev. D 104 (2021) 035005 | Connects lattice information on dark SU(N) deconfinement with the duration, energy budget and reach of future experiments. The link from lattice thermodynamics to stochastic spectra predates this exercise. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.11614) |
| Bigazzi, Caddeo, Cotrone & Paredes, 2020–21 | Uses a holographic SU(N) theory with matter to compute confinement and chiral-transition spectra. Some parameter choices reach PTA or interferometer bands. Our 90-point result cannot be generalized to those source families. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.08757) |
| Badger et al., Phys. Rev. D 107 (2023) 023511 | Uses O1–O3 data to constrain supercooled transitions and two particle models. Provides precedent for mapping a stochastic likelihood back onto microscopic parameters. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14707) |
| NANOGrav 15-year new-physics analysis and Fujikura et al., 2023 | The collaboration fits cosmological source models, including phase transitions, with an astrophysical background. Fujikura et al. examine a confining, nearly conformal sector with a new Yang–Mills field. Our PTA calculation uses the array sensitivity and supplies expected SNR only. [Collaboration paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16219) · [Confining model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17086) |
| Caprini et al., LISA Cosmology Working Group, JCAP 10 (2024) 020 | Forecasts LISA reconstruction with source templates, galactic and extragalactic foregrounds and model interpretation. Our instrument-only SNR gives an optimistic forecast under fixed noise. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03723) |

**What this study adds.** Two archived three-flavor grids are passed through the released LVK cross-spectrum likelihood, varying both density and support of the microscopic prior and reporting the update relative to that same prior. A separate benchmark checks the spectral shape against a published curve. The tables quantify how little these detector data change relative model probabilities on the tested support.

**Limits of the review.** The search covered primary articles and archives on prior dependence, LVK phase-transition inference, non-Abelian dark confinement, lattice-informed pure Yang–Mills, holographic confinement, PTA dark transitions and LISA reconstruction. This targeted review does not establish priority for this specific combination. A research claim of novelty would require a citation-chain and independent-implementation audit, and evidence that the result survives changing the underlying transition model and numerical resolution.
